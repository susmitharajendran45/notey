# notey
a cute but professional notepad just like the one's on our desks!


A tactile, lightweight tear-off notepad that lives quietly on your desktop.

Most note apps today feel like full database managers when all you really wanted was a quick scratchpad. **notey** brings back the satisfying feeling of jotting something down on paper, tearing it off when you're done, and starting fresh.


## Features

- **Tear-off experience:** Realistic tear sound, a little burst of confetti, and an immediate clean sheet.
- **Torn pages archive:** Every torn note automatically lands in an archive, searchable by date or content.
- **Always-on-top pin:** Keep your scratchpad visible while browsing or working across windows.
- **Paper styles & tints:** Cycle through ruled, dotted, grid, and blank paper with warm, cozy tones.
- **Micro-tools built right in:**
  - Inline arithmetic (type `25 * 4 =` and it calculates the answer)
  - Interactive checklists & resizable tables
  - Red strikeout pen to cross things off
  - Image paste & drag support
- **Link manager:** A quick drawer to save bookmarks you need for the day.
- **Clean export:** Export notes to clean PNG snapshots (no UI clutter) or raw Markdown (`.md`).
- **Light on RAM:** Built with Tauri v2—tiny footprint, no browser bloat.
